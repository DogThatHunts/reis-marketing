###### **Market Highlights** 

**CLOSINGS THIS PERIOD CLOSINGS, AUG '26 AVG PRICE, TRAILING 12 MO TOP BUILDER 5,812 766 $374,680 DR HORTON** L 3.0% YoY L 1.2% YoY 1,614 closings (28% share) 

In Aug '26, the Myrtle Beach Region recorded 766 new home closings, 3.0% more than the 744 closings recorded in Aug '25. Over the trailing twelve months, the average new home closing price in the Myrtle Beach Region was $374,680, up 1.2% year-over-year. 

During the 01/01/2026 - 08/31/2026 reporting period, the region posted 5,812 closings. Horry led all counties with 3,581 closings, 61.6% of the regional total. DR HORTON was the top-selling builder in the region with 1,614 closings, a 27.8% share of the market. LOGAN HOMES saw the largest year-over-year swing among the region's top builders, with closings up 55.5% (155 to 241). 

The most active communities in the Myrtle Beach Region this period were AUBERON WOODS (157 closings), BRUNSWICK FOREST (136 closings), and WATERSIDE (123 closings). New to the market this period: BAYVIEW, WINFIELD FARMS, and BLUE HERON RETREAT.
